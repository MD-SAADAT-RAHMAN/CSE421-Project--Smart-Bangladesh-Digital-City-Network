\section{Overview}

The implementation of the Smart Bangladesh Digital City Network was carried out in six major phases. This document records which of those phases each member of Group 01 worked on.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{The six major implementation phases}
\label{tab:phases}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Phase} & \textbf{Name} \\
\midrule
Phase 1 & Design on paper \\
Phase 2 & Build the topology in Packet Tracer \\
Phase 3 & Addressing \\
Phase 4 & DHCP \\
Phase 5 & Routing \\
Phase 6 & Servers and services \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\noindent
Preliminary planning, the backup routing demonstration and the final report were carried out jointly by all three members and are therefore not attributed to any individual.
